Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Zero Server-Side Data Collection (100% In-Browser Processing)

Your privacy is our utmost priority. Client-Side Image Compressor & Resizer is architected with a strict privacy-first principle: your image files are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server or external cloud storage.

All image processing operations (including decompression, downscaling, compression, and format encoding) are executed exclusively within your client web browser's RAM using standard HTML5 Canvas and OffscreenCanvas APIs.

2. Cookies and Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to collect non-personally identifiable information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Local Storage and Preferences

Our website utilizes browser localStorage solely to remember your UI preferences, such as your selected Light or Dark color theme. No sensitive or personally identifiable data is stored.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not collect, process, or sell personal data or uploaded media, our service is inherently compliant with European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) standards.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page.
